HOW TO USE (you, the human)
1. Open any image AI that can take more than one picture (ChatGPT, Gemini, Flux, etc.).
2. Attach:
   A) blueprint.png from this folder  — layout only
   B) a picture of YOUR character     — who to draw (art, OC, screenshot)
   C) optional: extra refs for outfit, hair, or style
3. Paste everything below the line as the prompt.
4. If the AI asks for size, pick square 1:1, 768x768.

This is a bust-up rest still, not phone portrait (9:16) and not widescreen (16:9).
The blueprint is 768x768.

================================================================
PROMPT FOR THE IMAGE AI — copy from here down
================================================================

Make ONE character still that can be used as a VTuber base / rest pose.

WHAT EACH IMAGE IS
- First attached image (blue-hair person on green): FORMAT BLUEPRINT only.
  Copy framing, crop, pose, camera distance, expression, lighting, and background from it.
  Do NOT copy that person's identity, hair color, eye color, or clothes unless no other character was given.
- Other attached image(s): the USER'S CHARACTER.
  This is who to draw. Copy their face, hair, eyes, body, outfit, accessories, colors, species traits, and art style.

OUTPUT SPEC
- Square 1:1. 768x768 pixels. PNG.
- Bust-up / chest-up only: head + neck + shoulders + upper chest. Same crop as the blueprint.
- Character centered. Face large and easy to read. Head about the same size in the frame as the blueprint.
- Facing the camera. Chin level. No head turn. No head tilt. Shoulders square to camera.
- Both eyes open, looking at the viewer.
- Small closed-mouth smile. Soft, not a grin, not a frown, teeth not showing.
- Solid flat chroma-key green background, same green as the blueprint, edge to edge.
  No gradient, no room, no floor, no props, no furniture, no particles.
- No hands. No arms reaching into frame. No full body. No walking pose.
- One character only. No other people, animals, or objects.
- No text, watermark, logo, caption, UI, arrows, labels, or borders.
- Even, simple lighting. Clean readable face. Do not cover the eyes with hair.

KEEP FROM THE USER'S CHARACTER
- Art style (even if it is more detailed than the blueprint)
- Face shape, hair, eyes, skin, outfit, accessories
- Any unique traits (ears, horns, glasses, etc.)

COPY FROM THE BLUEPRINT
- How much of the body is in frame
- Front-facing idle pose
- Small smile + open eyes looking forward
- Green screen
- Camera height and distance

If the user's picture is full-body, a selfie, or has a busy background, still output this bust-up green-screen still.
If their description and their picture disagree, follow the picture for looks and follow this prompt for pose and framing.
