WHAT THIS IS

The first image is a format blueprint for a VTuber reference still.
Copy its framing, pose, and background. Replace the character with yours.


COPY FROM THE BLUEPRINT (image 1)

- Aspect ratio: square 1:1 (not portrait 9:16, not landscape 16:9)
- Size: 768 x 768 pixels if the model lets you set resolution
- Background: solid flat chroma-key green, #00FF00, edge to edge
  no gradient, no shadows on the backdrop, no room, no props
- Crop: bust-up / chest-up only (head, neck, shoulders, upper chest)
- Camera: character centered, face large and easy to read
- Pose: facing the camera straight-on, chin level, no head tilt
- Eyes: both fully open, looking at the viewer
- Mouth: small closed-mouth smile, lips closed
- No hands, no arms in frame, no extra people, no animals
- No text, labels, watermarks, logos, or UI


COPY FROM THE USER'S CHARACTER (image 2+)

- Face, body, hair length/color/style
- Eyes, skin, outfit, accessories
- Art style (anime, painterly, chibi, etc.)
Keep that identity. Do not turn them into the blueprint girl.
